Die Mission

AI for owner run shops. Simply explained.

Big chains use data and forecasting every day. Small shops often work without it. On the Dot brings that clarity to the local counter and makes it easy to understand.

Warum es funktioniert

A simple system for more sales and less waste

01

Small menu, full focus

Shops offer a clear selection of ready made products, ready for preorders and exact timing.

02

Demand that makes sense

Simple forecasts help each shop order what it will sell and avoid overproduction.

03

Orders on time

Customers choose a delivery window. The shop sees the demand before the day gets busy.
